Cogsworth

A display, search, and multi-collection management app for Android devices.

Less deciding. More listening. Cogsworth brings your Discogs collection to your Android device, right beside the turntable.

Made for Android · Requires Android 8.0 or newer · Connected to Discogs

01 / BROWSE

Your shelves, on screen.

Choose your Discogs folders and browse by artist or album. Search by artist, title, label, or genre to find the record you have in mind.

02 / REDISCOVER

Let the next record find you.

Can’t decide what to play? Pick a random album from your selected collection and give a forgotten favourite another spin.

03 / DISPLAY

Give the artwork the room.

Leave Cogsworth beside your turntable. After a minute of inactivity, a scrolling mosaic of your album covers takes over the screen.

04 / REORGANIZE

From collection to collection....

Use multiple collections to keep track of the physical location of certain records? Select one or several collections to limit what you search on the current session. Easily move one or multiple records between collections.

Install Cogsworth.

  1. Download the latest Cogsworth APK on your Android device.
  2. Open Cogsworth.apk from your browser’s downloads.
  3. If Android asks, allow that browser to install unknown apps, then return to the installer.
  4. Tap Install. You can turn the browser’s install permission off again afterward.

First-run setup

Connect your collection.

Cogsworth connects directly to Discogs using your Discogs username and a personal access token. You only need to enter these once.

  1. Sign in to Discogs and open Settings → Developers.
  2. Generate a personal access token if you do not already have one.
  3. Open Cogsworth and enter your Discogs username and token.
  4. Choose one or more custom collection folders to start browsing.

Cogsworth does not display Discogs’ automatically generated “All” folder. Your token is encrypted on your Android device using Android Keystore.

Privacy

Your collection stays between you and Discogs.

Cogsworth sends your Discogs username, personal access token, and collection requests directly to Discogs so it can display and organize your collection. Album artwork is loaded from the image addresses supplied by Discogs.

Cogsworth has no developer-operated server and includes no advertising or analytics. It does not send your credentials, collection, or app activity to the Cogsworth developer or to any independent third party. Your username and encrypted token remain on your device until you clear them or remove the app.

This privacy policy applies to the Cogsworth Android app. Discogs processes information according to its own privacy policy.
